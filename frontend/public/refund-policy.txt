Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

At TrueMarriage, we aim to provide reliable and valuable matrimonial services to our users. This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains the terms applicable to payments made for our premium services, memberships, biodata services, profile boosts, and other paid features available on the platform.

By making a payment on TrueMarriage, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined below.

1. General Refund Policy

Most services offered through TrueMarriage are digital services that become available immediately after successful payment. Therefore, payments for digital services are generally non-refundable once the service or feature has been activated, delivered, downloaded, or used.

Users are encouraged to carefully review the service, pricing, subscription period, and features before completing a payment.

2. Premium Memberships

Payments made for premium memberships or subscription plans are generally non-refundable after the membership has been activated.

If you purchase a membership and experience a technical issue that prevents you from accessing the paid features, please contact our support team. We may review the issue and, where appropriate, provide an extension, restoration of access, or refund at our discretion.

Cancellation of a membership does not automatically result in a refund for the unused portion of the subscription period unless otherwise determined by TrueMarriage or required under applicable law.

3. Biodata Creation and Digital Products

Payments for premium biodata templates, customized biodata designs, downloadable documents, or other digital products are generally non-refundable once the product has been generated, delivered, downloaded, or made available to the user.

If you experience a technical problem, such as an inability to generate or download a purchased biodata due to an issue with our platform, please contact us so that we can investigate and resolve the issue.

4. Profile Boosts and Promotional Services

Payments for profile boosts, featured listings, priority placement, increased visibility, or similar promotional services are generally non-refundable once the service has been activated.

If a paid promotional service is not activated due to a technical error on our platform, please contact our support team. We will investigate the issue and may provide the service again, extend the service period, or issue an appropriate refund where applicable.

5. Duplicate Payments

If you have accidentally made the same payment more than once for the same service, please contact us with the relevant transaction details.

After verification, a duplicate payment may be refunded to the original payment method.

6. Failed Transactions

If your payment has been deducted from your bank account, card, UPI, or other payment method but the purchased service was not activated, please contact us.

We will verify the transaction with our payment processing system. If the transaction is confirmed as failed or unsuccessful and the amount was actually deducted, the amount will normally be refunded through the original payment method, subject to payment gateway and banking processing timelines.

7. Unauthorized Transactions

If you believe that a payment was made through your account without your authorization, please contact us as soon as possible with the transaction details.

We may request additional information to verify the transaction and investigate the matter.

8. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered in circumstances including:

Duplicate payment for the same service.

Payment deducted but the purchased service was not activated due to a technical issue.

Technical failure that prevents delivery of a purchased digital service.

Unauthorized transaction, subject to verification.

Any other situation where a refund is required under applicable law.

Refunds will not normally be provided for:

Change of mind after purchasing a digital service.

Failure to use a purchased membership or service.

Unused subscription time.

Services that have already been delivered, generated, downloaded, or substantially used.

Profile boosts or promotional services that have already been activated.

Incorrect information entered by the user while creating a profile or biodata.

Dissatisfaction with matrimonial matches, responses, communication, or results obtained through the platform.

9. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team with the following information:

Registered name

Registered email address or mobile number

Transaction/order ID

Date of payment

Name of the purchased service

Amount paid

Reason for the refund request

Any relevant screenshots or supporting information

Refund requests should be submitted as soon as possible after the issue occurs.

Our team will review the request and communicate the outcome after verification.

If a refund is approved, it will generally be processed to the original payment method used for the transaction.

TrueMarriage is not responsible for delays caused by banks, payment gateways, card networks, or other third-party financial service providers.

Users may request cancellation of their subscription or disable automatic renewal, where applicable, through the available account or subscription settings.

Cancellation will prevent future renewal charges but does not automatically make the current subscription period refundable.

Any refund associated with cancellation will be handled according to this Refund & Cancellation Policy and applicable law.
